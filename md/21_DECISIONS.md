# DECISIONS LOG

**1. The 2D Fallback:**
Decided: The site will retain a fully functional 2D DOM portfolio underneath the WebGL canvas. If WebGL fails, or `prefers-reduced-motion` is active, the 3D canvas is deleted and the 2D site is revealed.

**2. Private vs Public Context:**
Decided: The family-responsibility story is strictly private. It informs the architectural tone (bearing weight alone, massive concrete, resilience) but is never written as text.

**3. Zero-Build Architecture:**
Decided: No NPM, no Webpack, no Vite. Pure ES modules over CDN.

**4. The Architectural Reset (Phase 6.6):**
Decided: The "straight infinite hallway" prototype (Phase 6.3-6.5) is abandoned in favor of a curated, asymmetric architectural space with specific zones.

**5. The Entry & Identity Reveal (Phase 6.6 Refinement):**
Decided: The entry will be absolute darkness with a subtle atmospheric rumble. The environment responds to the first user input with a *restrained physical disturbance* (dust settling). Identity is discovered as a moving light source casts shadows from abstract concrete blocks that align to spell "ASHISH". No extra visual fluff (bloom, shake, flares) is permitted. 

**6. The Contact Interaction (Phase 6.6 Refinement):**
Decided: The physical massive door metaphor remains, but contacting the owner must be completely effortless via a simple click/tap on a sleek terminal. No heavy scrolling required for conversion.

**7. Sound Design:**
Decided: Sound will be cinematic and environmental (dust settling, heavy mechanical shifts). No annoying synthetic arcade oscillators. Must be non-intrusive.
